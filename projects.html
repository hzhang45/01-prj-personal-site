About Sharpen

Sharpen is a collaborated project I created with a friend over the summer of my Junior Year of Highschool.

It is a dungeon diving board game where the world has been pixelated, the king has ordered many adventurers to find the three artifacts to change the world back, the first adventurer to gather all three and restore the world will become the next king.

The Objective of the game is to explore three dungeon levels each with a Large Chest containing an ARTIFACT, the first player to gather all three ARTIFACTS wins the game.


Components

  • Game Board 1
    (2 sided, 3 maps)
  • Map Tiles 120
  • Player Pieces 5
  • Dice 2
    D6 and D8
  • Character Cards 5
  • Ability Cards 25
  • Item Cards 36
  • Key Cards 15
  • Artifact Cards 3
  • Monster Cards 42
  • Gold Pieces
    1, 3, 5, and 10

Rules

Movement

A character can move as many tiles based on their speed.
If a character has 4 speed1, they have to move 1-4 tiles this round.
You can not move diagonally unless an ability or item allows you to.

Exploration

When a character moves on to an unexplored tile, they flip it over, if it is a ground tile, they can keep moving, if this tile is not a ground tile, they will need to stop and do the action, and only keep moving after they complete the action.

Round Phases

Beginning Phase - You can enter the shop, and buy or sell items, buy an ability, or reroll abilities.

Action Phase - You can move, use items/artifacts, abilities, to explore and move around the map.

End Phase - You can enter the shop again, only to buy or sell items.

Shop Use

There are four things you can do while in the shop, Buy Items, Sell Items, Buy Abilities, and Reroll Abilities.

Buying Items is 3 gold, you get two random item cards and choose one to keep

Selling Items is 1 gold.

Buying Abilities is 5 gold, you will get a random ability that you do not already have.

Re-rolling Abilities is 5 gold, you will get a random ability that you do not already have, this ability can not be the ability you are re-rolling.

Inventory

Golds, keys and artifacts should be laid out for everyone to see. Items are private in a player`s hand, but you can show your hand to other players if you want to.

A player's hand can not exceed the 3 card limit, unless an ability allows you to.

Every item can only be used once. Shuffle it back into the item deck after use.
